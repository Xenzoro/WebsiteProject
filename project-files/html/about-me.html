Hands-On Work

I love the whole design cycle. From random concepts to the nitty-gritty, time-consuming code, whether that's low-level assembly or a high-level web app. I keep that same hands-on philosophy in hardware. I taught myself to solder PCBs on gaming controllers like Xbox and PS4/5s as a side gig. I replaced stock joysticks with TMR (Tunnel Magnetoresistance) joysticks; these new modules are stick drift-free! I also recalibrated them myself. When I'm branching into a new topic or hobby, I use AI as a tutor to expand my knowledge of what I know and want to know. I recognize it can be used as a crutch, but my own discipline has kept me grounded; my ideas have come from my own head, and my own dreams push me towards a future I'll build myself.
